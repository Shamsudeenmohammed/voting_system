Role guide

  • Election commission chair — oversees the assigned institution's elections: lifecycle, readiness, schedules, polling stations, officials, escalations and (where the governance policy assigns it) certification. Chairs may appoint election officials and observers only.
  • Institution election administrator — full control of the tenant's elections, including approvals and certification.
  • Election official — configuration, registry and lifecycle execution.
  • Auditor — read-only: results, monitoring and audit-chain verification.
  • Observer — public data only; no console access.

Only the roles you are permitted to grant appear in the forms above. The Super Admin is never scoped to an institution and may grant any role anywhere.
