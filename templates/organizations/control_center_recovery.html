Recovery actions

Explicit, audited interventions for the rare cases where a tenant-level mistake has locked somebody out or left the console inconsistent. Each action records the actor, the target, the documented reason and the before/after value in the hash-chained audit log.

These actions bypass the normal tenant workflow. Use them only to restore access or consistency — never to influence an election outcome. Ballot secrecy is unaffected: no recovery action can read individual selections.
